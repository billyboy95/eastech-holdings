Pay student fees

Pay with Yoco, or by EFT to the school’s ABSA account. The card form needs the amount and a student number or invoice.

Test mode is on. No payment is sent until a Yoco test payment link or a checkout endpoint is saved in shared/config.js.

For an EFT, use the reference “Name of Student & Grade”.

Connecting Yoco

Test mode is on in shared/config.js. Paste a Yoco test payment link into YOCO_PAYMENT_LINK_URL, or point YOCO_CHECKOUT_ENDPOINT at your server. The server calls Yoco Checkout at https://payments.yoco.com/api/checkouts with the secret key and returns redirectUrl. YOCO_PUBLIC_KEY and YOCO_SECRET_KEY stay placeholders in this public file. The browser does not send the secret.

A payment link may include {amount}, {amountCents}, and {reference}. The checkout endpoint receives the amount in cents, the reference, this campus, and the return addresses.
